1. What you're buying

A personalised print about the address you give us, made to order. Each print shows historic and modern maps, a written history of the street, a timeline, recorded sales on the street (without house numbers) and your message. The exact content depends on what the records show for your street.

2. Ordering and your proof

Your order is accepted when payment is taken. We email a digital proof within 2 working days. You can ask for reasonable changes to the wording, style and frame colour. Nothing is printed until you approve the proof by email. If you do not reply within 30 days, we will contact you before printing or cancelling.

3. Prices and payment

Prices are in pounds sterling and include delivery to UK addresses. We are not VAT-registered, so no VAT is added. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not see or store your card details.

4. Delivery

Approved prints are printed and dispatched by our UK print partner, usually within 3 working days, and delivered in 1–3 working days after that. Delivery dates are estimates; we will tell you about any delay.

5. Accuracy

We take facts only from named sources and list them on the print. Old records can disagree; if your print contains a factual error that you tell us about within 60 days, we will correct it and reprint free.

See our returns policy. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights.

7. Content and permissions

You confirm that you have the right to ask for a print about the address you give, and that your message does not infringe anyone's rights. We may refuse orders that are offensive or that appear to target someone without their knowledge.
