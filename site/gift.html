Questions

Gift voucher questions

How long is a voucher valid?

12 months from the day you buy it. The date is printed on the voucher.

Can they choose the style and frame?

Yes. They pick one of four styles (Heritage, Modern, Midnight or Sage) when they redeem, and for a framed print they can choose black, white or natural wood when they approve their proof.

Can it be for a house they haven't moved into yet?

Yes. Any address in England and Wales works, so they can redeem it before or after moving day.

What if I lose the voucher?

Reply to the email your voucher came in (or email hello@housealmanac.co.uk from the address you used) and we'll resend it.

Can I change my mind?

Yes: an unused voucher can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Just reply to your voucher email.

What's on the print?

Their street on a historic Ordnance Survey map and the same spot today, a short history of the street, a timeline, recorded sales on the street (never their own home's price) and a message of their choice. See a sample.
