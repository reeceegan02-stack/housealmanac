Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

House Almanac is a trading name of Reece Egan-Sheath, a sole trader of Flat 1422, New Victoria Building, Manchester M4 4LN, United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@housealmanac.co.uk.

What we collect

When you order: your name, email address, delivery address, the address the print is about, any names and message you want on the print, and your order and payment status. We never receive your full card details.

Why we use it

To research and make your print, send you a proof, deliver your order, answer your questions and keep the business records the law requires. Our lawful basis is performing our contract with you, and our legal obligations for tax records. We will only send you marketing if you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the business: Stripe (payments), Prodigi (printing and delivery), Cloudflare (website and email forwarding), Google (email) and Anthropic (AI tools that help us research and draft your print). They process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms. Some may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

Print files and proofs are deleted 90 days after delivery. Order and payment records are kept for 6 years for tax purposes.

Public records on your print

Prints use public datasets such as HM Land Registry Price Paid Data. We leave house numbers off sales charts and never show the sales of the home the print is about.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe's checkout uses cookies needed for secure payment.
